If wp-admin still redirects after uploading root .htaccess:

1. Check WordPress URLs in public_html/blog/wp-config.php (above "That's all"):
   define('WP_HOME', 'https://appsdeddy.com/blog');
   define('WP_SITEURL', 'https://appsdeddy.com/blog');

2. In phpMyAdmin (Hostinger), table wp_options:
   siteurl = https://appsdeddy.com/blog
   home    = https://appsdeddy.com/blog
   (table prefix may be wp_ or different)

3. Ensure public_html/blog/.htaccess exists (WordPress standard rules).
   Re-save Permalinks in WP admin if you can reach it once.

4. Try: https://appsdeddy.com/blog/wp-login.php directly (not /wp-admin first).
